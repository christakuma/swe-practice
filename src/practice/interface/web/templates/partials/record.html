{# What the history says about one exercise, as a chip. Nothing at all for an exercise never attempted, which is most of a library most of the time: a row of a hundred "not started" chips would be a hundred marks that mean nothing, and the absence already says it. **One partial for every list**, because the browse page, the due queue and a track are the same exercise listed for three different reasons, and the one thing that must not differ between them is whether you have done it. **Said in words, with the date.** "passed 2026-10-09" rather than a tick: the question this answers is *which have I done and when*, a tick answers half of it, and a status told only in a glyph and a colour is one a printout loses. The date is the attempt's own — the same `finished_at` `/progress` prints in its history — so the two pages cannot disagree about which day it was.

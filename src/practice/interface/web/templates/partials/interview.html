{# The interview banner: what this attempt's rules are, and how much of the clock is left. **Server-rendered on load and not a live countdown**, which is a decision rather than a missing feature. A JavaScript timer would be the one number on this page that nothing grades — it would tick past zero and change nothing, because what decides whether an attempt ran over is `suggest_grade` reading `elapsed` against the deadline at submission. A clock that looks authoritative and is decorative is worse than a stated fact, and it is the shape of defect Phase 13 found in the schedule panel's ease factor (PLAN.md §16). So this says what is true when the page is drawn, and says that is what it is. Reloading is how you ask again. **The deadline does not stop you submitting.** Refusing a late submission would destroy the work, and the core bet says grade the end state: being over is recorded and read off the finished attempt, not enforced by a gate (ADR 0020). #}
